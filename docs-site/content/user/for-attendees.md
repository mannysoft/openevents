This page is for people buying tickets. It explains what happens from finding an event to walking through the door.

## Find an event

- The event list is at **/events** and shows only upcoming events that are on sale to the public.
- An organiser's page, at **/o/the-organiser-name**, lists everything that organiser is currently selling.
- Organisers usually share a direct link to a single event, which looks like **/e/the-event-name**.

The event page shows the date and time in the event's own timezone, the venue, a description and the ticket types with their prices.

## Step 1: choose your tickets

Select **Get tickets** to start. Use **+** and **−** to set the number of each ticket type; the subtotal updates as you go.

Each ticket type may have a minimum and maximum per order, and a limited number remaining — the page shows how many are left when there is a limit. When your selection is right, select **Continue**.

If the button is missing and the page says tickets are not on sale at the moment, the event is either not open for sales yet or has sold out.

## Step 2: your details

Enter your own **First name**, **Last name** and **Email** — this is who the order belongs to and where the emails go.

Then fill in one set of details for **each attendee**, one block per ticket. Each needs a first name, last name and email, so everyone gets their own ticket and QR code.

Some organisers ask extra questions here. A question marked with a red asterisk is required and must be answered before you can pay.

If you have a **discount code**, type it into the discount box. Only one code can be used per order.

Select **Continue to payment**.

## Step 3: pay

The final step shows your order reference and the total, plus a payment form provided by Stripe. Enter your payment details and select **Pay**.

- Your card details go straight to Stripe; the organiser never sees them.
- If your bank asks you to confirm the payment, complete that check and you will be returned to the site.
- If a card is declined, a message appears under the form. Try another card or contact your bank.
- If your tickets are free, or a discount covers the whole order, there is no payment step at all.

Do not close the browser while the payment is being processed.

## After you pay

You land on a confirmation page at **/orders/your-order-reference**. It shows:

- **Payment received** once the payment has cleared.
- **Awaiting payment confirmation** if Stripe has not reported the payment yet. Wait a moment and select **Refresh status**.
- **Refunded** or **Cancelled** if the organiser has changed the order.

The page lists every ticket with its reference, a **View ticket** link and a **PDF** download.

## Your ticket

Each ticket page has a QR code and shows the attendee name, ticket type, reference, and the event date and venue. You can:

- Show the QR code on your phone at the door.
- Select **Download PDF** to save or print a copy.

:::tip
Screenshot your QR code or download the PDF before you travel. Venue wifi and mobile signal are often unreliable exactly when you need them.
:::

## At the door

The organiser scans your QR code or types your reference. If it works you are admitted; if the scanner says the ticket was already used, speak to the door staff — it may have been scanned by mistake.

Refunded and cancelled tickets will not be accepted.

## Emails you will receive

| Email | When |
| --- | --- |
| Your tickets for the event | Sent automatically after a successful payment, listing your tickets and references. |
| Your ticket for the event | A single ticket, if the organiser resends it to you. |
| Organiser messages | Updates such as venue changes or joining instructions. |

If nothing arrives, check your spam or junk folder and make sure the address on the order is correct. To reach the organiser, reply to one of their emails about the event.

## Refunds and changes

There is no self-service refund or name change. Contact the organiser — reply to your ticket email — and they can refund the order or edit an attendee's name and email.

A partial refund leaves your tickets valid; a full refund cancels them and they will no longer scan at the door.

:::note
Ticket availability, prices, questions and refund policies are all set by the organiser. The platform processes the payment but cannot change an organiser's decision.
:::
